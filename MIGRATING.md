# Migrating to 0.4.66

- **The 44px touch floor needs no scope class (`touch-floor`, `pi-chat`, `app-shell`, `viewer-frame`, `feedback`, `theme`, `shadcn/ui` Button; D-1770).** A new fileless registry item, `touch-floor`, carries one global rule: under 768px or on a coarse pointer, every `[data-slot="button"]`, `toggle`, `toggle-group-item`, `group/button` and `group/toggle` is at least `--control-min` (2.75rem) high and wide, on a page with no `.pi-chat`, `.app-shell` or `.boring-viewer`, in a portaled Popover, and on your own older Button copy. What you will see: `size="sm"`, `xs`, `icon-sm`, `icon-xs` Buttons, Toggles and ToggleGroupItems outside the kit's blocks are 44px on a phone, as the kit's own already were; a fine pointer from md up is unchanged (24, 20, 24, 20 and 32 for a small Toggle). Opt out as before: `data-density="compact"` on the control or a container (the rule does not apply there, so sizes stay the desktop ones even on touch); a Button `size="inline"` (`data-size="inline"`) is skipped. The scoped copies in `pi-chat`, `app-shell` and `viewer-frame` are removed (the global rule replaces them); the rule lives only in `touch-floor`, which `pi-chat`, `app-shell`, `viewer-frame`, `feedback` and `theme` list in `registryDependencies`, so an app that installs any of them gets it, whether or not it took the `theme` (a branded app keeps its own tokens; without `--control-min` the rule falls back to 2.75rem on touch). `shadcn add <block>.json` and `boring-ui-blocks update` (which re-adds each installed block with `--overwrite`) both resolve and merge it; the bundle's `blocks.css` merges every item. Button now sets `data-size={size}` (both primitive copies). `feedback` keeps its own 640px rule.
- **New block `file-pick`: `FilePick`, a "choose a file" Button with an optional drop strip (D-1783).** For the hand-made hidden `<input type=file>` behind a Button. Props: `onFiles(File[])`, `onRefused({ file, reason }[])` (`reason`: `'type' | 'size' | 'count'`; the app writes the words), `accept` (MIME, `image/*`, `.ext`; also applied to picks and drops), `multiple`, `maxSize` (bytes), `disabled`, `busy` (the kit's spinner, `aria-busy`, `aria-disabled` instead of `disabled` so the variant keeps its colours; no picks or drops), `variant` and `size` (the Button's), `drop` (a dashed strip with a hint line: `hint` or `labels.dropHint` / `dropHintMore`), `children` (the label).
