## 2026-09-16 — Real cases, a frozen agreement, and delivery in service

**Trigger:** Jérôme's BORING process and journal documented two weeks of iteration without convergence, ambiguity about which revision had been approved, and a product that could be built without being usable at its real address.

**Change:** discovery begins with the existing terrain and three lived cases, including the moment the old way wins under pressure. The agreement has fixed measurable, scope, acceptance, limit, and question sections. Every shown sketch or preview names its revision. Keeping the sketch freezes that agreement; later needs open a new intent. Post-agreement changes are classified by who introduced them and whether they need an explicit yes. A kept app is delivered with its address, phone home-screen guidance, and a plain recovery phrase.
