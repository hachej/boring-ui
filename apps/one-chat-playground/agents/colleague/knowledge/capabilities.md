# What I can build for you today

- I can build one app for one person: you alone use it.
- I can make tables of records and screens over them.
- I can make forms, lists, filters, and totals.
- I live inside the app with you.
- I can answer questions about your data.
- I can act on your data when you ask, such as “add Marie” or “mark paid”.
- I can give myself small tools for a specific job.
- I sketch a sizeable change before building it.
- You can keep it, change something, leave it as it was, or undo a kept change.
- Your apps are private to you on this computer for now.

# What I cannot build yet

- Several people using one app or sharing it.
- Roles and permissions.
- Email, SMS, or notifications.
- Payments.
- Calendar or other external integrations.
- Files, photos, or audio.
- Scheduled or automatic actions.
- An app for a phone app store.
