# 07 — Test the uncertainty that could change the decision

## Source-backed principle

Product Talk separates interviews that uncover opportunities from assumption tests that evaluate potential solutions [S08](sources.md#s08). Moderated usability testing observes likely users attempting tasks and reveals comprehension or interaction problems [S16](sources.md#s16). A positive conversation does not perform either a feasibility check or a usability test.

## Boring PM application

For the first slice, list what must be true, what evidence supports it, what would happen if it were wrong, and how to learn cheaply. Test the assumption that can invalidate the most important decision. Run multiple tests only when they resolve different risks.

| Uncertainty | Suitable evidence-producing activity | What it does not establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Users have this problem | Relevant task observation and recent cases | Willingness to adopt the proposed solution |
| Users will use or buy it | Realistic pilot, trial commitment, or permitted purchase experiment | Long-term retention or broad demand |
| Users can operate it | Representative task attempt with a prototype | Backend feasibility or business viability |
| Core integration works | Narrow spike using the real interface and realistic inputs | A finished or supportable product |
| Expert logic generalizes | Review of varied cases, including exceptions | Statistical validity beyond the evaluated cases |
| Operation is sustainable | Cost/workload estimates and operator review | Production reliability without observation |

## Write the experiment before seeing the result

Record an assumption, participant/input selection, procedure, expected observations, success/failure/inconclusive criteria, timebox, and decision rule. Thresholds are proposed product choices unless independently justified. Decide what a failed result would change so the test is not merely a demonstration.

Use relevant variation rather than a universal participant count. A few task trials can identify substantial usability problems, but cannot estimate a market conversion rate precisely. Record the sample, context, missing perspectives, and limitations.

## Run a task trial

Give the participant a goal and realistic input. Avoid teaching the interface while measuring whether it is understandable. Observe completion, mistakes, recovery, and requested help. After the attempt, ask about surprising actions and expectations. Label coached success separately from independent success.

Record `planned`, `running`, `passed`, `failed`, `inconclusive`, or `blocked`, with the actual result and artifact. Do not backfill a success criterion after seeing the data.

## Exit and failure patterns

A result should change confidence or a product decision. Record whether to proceed, narrow, redesign, investigate, or stop. A consciously accepted uncertainty remains an uncertainty, with scope and consequences documented. Use [the experiment record](../assets/templates/05-experiment.md).
