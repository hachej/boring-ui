# Bathroom image-generation brief

## Primary prompt

Create a **photorealistic high-end architectural interior render** of the compact bathroom shown in `01-floor-plan-bathroom.png` (approximately **5.56 m²**, long rectangular shape). Preserve the floor-plan geometry and circulation exactly.

Camera is positioned at the bathroom entrance, at approximately 1.55 m eye height, looking toward the vanity wall. Use a realistic 24–28 mm architectural lens, straight vertical lines, landscape 16:10 composition.

### Required layout — the floor plan has priority

- Keep the doorway and its clear opening unobstructed.
- Place the wall-hung WC on the left side, as shown on the plan.
- Place a double-basin vanity on the far wall, centered as shown on the plan.
- Keep the bathtub running lengthwise along the right-hand wall, with an overhead shower and a frameless clear-glass bath screen.
- Maintain a comfortable clear circulation strip through the middle.
- Do **not** replace the bathtub with a walk-in-only shower.
- Do **not** enlarge or reshape the room.

### Elements to combine from the references

1. From `02-reference-mirrors.png`, use the **mirror design only**: two separate, tall, narrow, rounded-corner rectangular mirrors, one above each basin, with very slim dark-bronze or black frames. Keep their elegant vertical proportions.
2. From `03-reference-dark-wood-wall.png`, use the **material and atmosphere**: deep charcoal microcement walls and ceiling, a warm vertical teak/wood-look slatted feature in the bath/shower wet zone, recessed illuminated niches, frameless glass and matte-black shower hardware.
3. Use `04-composition-guide.png` only as a rough layout/palette guide. The final output must look significantly more realistic and refined.

### Supporting design

- Floating warm-walnut double vanity, visually light and sized to the room.
- Thin off-white stone or warm ivory countertop.
- Two simple white vessel basins.
- Matte-black taps and shower fittings.
- Warm wood-look porcelain floor tile suitable for a bathroom, with subtle natural variation.
- Concealed warm LED wash around the wood feature and niches.
- Soft 2700–3000 K lighting, including subtle mirror lighting and small recessed ceiling spots.
- Sophisticated, calm, tactile, modern atmosphere; dark but not gloomy.
- Realistic dimensions, construction details, reflections, shadows and material textures.
- Minimal styling: one folded towel and one restrained plant or ceramic object only.

## Negative prompt

Do not change the floor-plan shape. No oversized bathroom. No bathtub across the far wall. No freestanding tub. No walk-in-only shower. No second shower. No vanity or furniture in the doorway or door-swing area. No extra cabinets blocking circulation. No extra toilet or bidet. No invented window. No single wide mirror. No round mirror. No arched mirror. No rustic farmhouse room. No white marble everywhere. No bright gold plumbing. No glossy black tile. No excessive decor, toiletries, clutter, people, text, labels, fisheye distortion, tilted verticals, duplicated fixtures, malformed sinks or impossible reflections.

## Recommended outputs

Generate at least four variants:

1. **Hero view:** doorway toward vanity, showing WC left and bath/shower right.
2. **Vanity emphasis:** slightly tighter frame prioritizing the two mirrors.
3. **Wet-zone emphasis:** camera shifted slightly left to reveal the wood feature and niches.
4. **Brighter variant:** same materials and layout, with more balanced daylight-like fill while retaining warm accents.

Preferred aspect ratio: **16:10 or 3:2 landscape**. Minimum width: **1536 px**.
