- **`FloatingComposer` takes the window content `AmbientChat` had (new; additive, each opt-in): `embedded`, `artifactPanel`, `artifacts` with `artifactTarget`, `headerTools`, `headerActions`, `onDock`, `onOpenFull`, `onFeedback`.** Same names and meaning, so `AmbientChat` forwards them and no longer falls back to the old window for them. `<FloatingComposer agent={agent} appId="site" headerActions={[{ id: 'share', label: 'Share', icon: ShareIcon, onSelect }]} onOpenFull={id => router.push(`/chat/${id}`)} onFeedback={(reply, value) => rate(reply.key, value)} artifactPanel={(artifact, api) => <MyViewer artifact={artifact} api={api} />} />`. What differs from the old window: `headerActions` are the shell's `ScreenHeaderAction` (`icon` required; `AmbientChat` maps its `BlockAction`s: a label-only one shows its label, `placement: 'menu'` stays in « … », `disabled` does nothing; `pressed` and `destructive` are not carried); `onDock` and `onOpenFull` are actions in the window's title row (« Dock chat », « Open in full chat »), not a Dock button and a button on each reply; an artifact opens in a window beside the floating bar (the whole screen on a phone), not by widening the bar; `embedded` draws the thread and its field in the container, with no title row (so no header actions or Dock there; the host supplies the header). Words: `dockChat`, `openFull`, `goodResponse`, `badResponse`, `artifactPanel` in `agent.words` (English and French built in; `AmbientChat` passes the app's `labels.dock`, `openFull`, `goodResponse`, `badResponse` when it gives them). Also: `ScreenHeaderAction.placement: 'menu'` (one that stays in « … »), `AgentSpec.headerTools` and `AgentSpec.artifactPanel`; `headerActions` now also show in the floating or docked tray's title row of any `composer="bar"`.
