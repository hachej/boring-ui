- **`signin`: `mcpPerson` accepts Studio's pass for an app linked to Studio (additive; nothing to change).** With the `studio` option the app already passes, `mcpPerson` now also takes Studio's 5-minute EdDSA pass (issuer `studio.origin`, audience `studio.app`) beside the app's own OAuth tokens, checked against Studio's `/.well-known/jwks.json` through `studio.fetch`, and answers the app's person (the `studio` account of the pass's `sub`, else the user of its e-mail) with the role `roleOf` works out at that request; any other pass is the ordinary `mcpRefusal` 401. An app that wrote its own check of Studio's pass deletes it. `personOf` still never takes a pass. Feature: `features/signin.md` (`signin-studio-pass`).
