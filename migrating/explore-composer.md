- **`app-shell`: `composer="explore"`, the composer for screens people read (additive, opt-in; visible only with the prop).** `<AppShell composer="explore" agent={agent} context={{ title: 'Orders, last 30 days', detail: '4 rows' }} />`: closed on every visit, so the page keeps its whole width and nothing sits at the bottom; a small « Ask » (fr « Demander ») tab at the bottom right opens a right panel (Ctrl or Cmd + J toggles, Esc closes, focus goes into the field and back to the tab). The panel pushes the page from 1100 px, overlays it below that, and is the bottom sheet on a phone. Its width is resizable (drag the side edge, or the arrow keys) and remembered per app and person (`boring-ui:sidebar-explore-width:<app>:<person>`); it still starts closed. The closed tab carries the icon's signals: a dot when a reply came while closed, the ring while a decision waits. It replaces `floatingComposer`'s icon (not combined) and hides the header trigger. The optional `context` prop (`{ title, detail? }`) is shown at the top of the panel under « You are looking at » and sent with each turn as `readAgentContext().params.lookingAt`; without it nothing is shown or sent. No app change is needed unless you opt in. Reinstall with `boring-ui-blocks add app-shell` (new file `explore.tsx`; `AgentComposer` gains `'explore'`, `AppShellProps` gains `context`, text keys `ask`, `lookingAt`, `resizeExplore`). Tests: `test/contracts/explore-composer.test.mjs`, `test/contracts/explore-composer-browser.test.mjs`.
