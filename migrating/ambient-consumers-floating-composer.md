- **`FloatingComposer` replaces `AmbientChat` in the kit's own hosts (`placed-agent`, the dock, workspace and studio examples).** `PlacedAgentWorkspace` now floats its collapsed chat as `FloatingComposer` (open on first show, its place remembered under `${storageKey}.ambient` in `localStorage`, Dock chat as a title-row action), not `AmbientChat`. A host that mounted `AmbientChat` for a floating chat moves with `<FloatingComposer agent={{ controller, chatProps }} appId="…" composer={{ ...FLOATING_COMPOSER_PRESET, defaultLevel: 'open', at: { remember: 'local' } }} onDock={dock} />` (props `headerActions`, `headerTools`, `onDock`, `onOpenFull`, `onFeedback`, `artifactPanel`, `artifacts`, `artifactTarget` and `embedded` keep their names; `labels`, `tools`, `attachments` and the rest of the chat's props go in `chatProps`; a `headerActions` entry without an `icon` shows its label; `onDock` adds « Dock chat » on a phone too, where the old window hid it: pass it only when the host can dock, for example `{...(api.canDock ? { onDock: api.dock } : {})}`). Differences: the standalone composer reports its open chat to the model as `floating` (it said `docked`); the agent's `present` opens in the composer's pane by itself (the old window never auto-opened); `variant="contrast"`, `state`/`onStateChange` (use `composer.level` with `on.level`), the `notifications` store and `systemNotifications` have no counterpart. `AmbientChat` itself stays until its last consumer is moved.
