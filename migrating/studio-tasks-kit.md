- **`list-row`, `inline-question`, `kpi-stat`, `expert-overview`: Studio tasks parts (additive).** `ListRow` takes `active` (`aria-current="true"` and the selected wash) and `action`, a node such as `<CiteButton reference={…} />` drawn beside the row button, never inside it, shown on hover or focus and always on touch. `InlineQuestion` takes `detail` (one muted line of why under the question) and `tone="info"` (the blue tint, beside `attention`). `KpiStat` takes `trend` (readings, drawn as a sparkline; fewer than two draw nothing) and `trendLabel`. `expert-overview` now exports `SegmentBar` (`segments`, `current`, `label`, `turn`), the bar `ExpertTrack` draws, with a segment's `detail` shown on hover only. Every prop is optional and today's markup is unchanged without them. Re-run `npx boring-ui-blocks add list-row inline-question kpi-stat expert-overview` to pick them up; nothing else to change.
