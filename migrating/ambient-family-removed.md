- **The Ambient family is removed (`pi-ambient`: `AmbientChat`, `AmbientWindow`, `AgentNotifications`, `browser-notify`).** Mount `FloatingComposer` (`app-shell`) instead. Not carried over: the draggable folded pill (the folded icon only restores on a tap; the bar's grip moves the bar), the phone hiding Dock chat (pass `onDock` only when the host can dock), `variant="contrast"`, `state`/`defaultState`/`onStateChange` (use `composer.level` with `on.level`), `systemNotifications` (browser notifications while the page is hidden: no replacement in the kit; an app calls the `Notification` API itself), and the `notifications` store with its toasts for background conversations and tasks. For background-task toasts use the kit's own feed, `watchConversation(controller, store, { conversationId, title })` or `useRunNotifications` from `@hachej/boring-ui-kit/chat-react`, and give `FloatingComposer` the result as `notices` (`AppShellNotice[]`, drawn by the shell's `NoticeHost`: one at a time above the bar, `autoDismissMs`, `maxToasts`). A hand-copied `components/pi-ambient/*` keeps working until the app removes it; `boring-ui-blocks` no longer lists the `pi-ambient` item.
