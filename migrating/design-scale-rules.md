- **`boring-ui-blocks design`: five new rules, `--changed` without files, `--jsonl` (additive, apps may fail on their next check).** `design-z-index` (raw `z-10`, `z-[45]`, `zIndex: 5`, `z-index: 40`: use `z-sheet` and the other named layers or `z-[var(--z-*)]`; `app.css` now declares `--z-dropdown`, `--z-composer`, `--z-sheet`, `--z-overlay`, `--z-toast`, `--z-feedback`), `design-type-scale` (`text-base` and larger unless `theme.core.css` declares `--text-<step>`), `design-gap-not-space` (`space-x-*`, `space-y-*`: use `gap-*`), `design-screen-padding` (no `p-*`/`px-*` on `ScreenBody`, its direct child or a screen root) and `design-has-design-md` (an app root with a `package.json` needs a `DESIGN.md`). An app with existing hits freezes them in `design.exceptions.json`. `design <dir> --changed` with no file checks what git reports changed; `--jsonl` prints one `{file,line,rule,level,fix}` line per hit. The feedback, viewer-frame, pi-chat and pi-ambient blocks use the named layers and `gap` (same look, no action needed).
