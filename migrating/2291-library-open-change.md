- **`AgentWorkspace`: `onLibraryOpenChange` (additive).** Optional `onLibraryOpenChange?: (open: boolean) => void` is called with the new value once per change of the Library's open state, in every `libraryPlacement` and on a phone, and from the embedded bar (`WorkspaceBar` takes the same prop). Not called on mount. There is no controlled counterpart; nothing to change unless you want to observe it. Re-run `boring-ui-blocks update pi-app` to get it.
