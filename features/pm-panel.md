# « Améliorer l'app »: the PM panel and the shared PM composer

The product manager inside any app (spec 2, boards C1–C7) and in Studio's conversation column (spec 1, boards D1, D7, D7b, D7c, D8, D9): one composer with « Montrer » to point at the app while speaking, numbered pins, timestamped chips and a review before sending. Specs: `/home/ubuntu/projects/_work/pm-spec`. Compare images: `.agent-handoff/pm-panel/compare/<board>.png`.

## Sub-features

- `pm-composer`: `PmComposer`, one component with a `touch` (app) and a `desktop` (Studio) preset, FR/EN strings (`pmTextFr`, `pmTextEn`) and theme tokens (`--pm-*` in the registry theme). `registry/pm-panel/pm-composer.tsx`, `pm-text.ts`.
- `pm-strip`: the point strip in place of the composer: `point.idle` (hint · mic · « ✓ Terminer »), `point.rec` select (toggle · hint · timer · « ✓ Terminer »), navigate (no hint). No pause and no × in the strip (Julien 2026-10-28): the timer opens the kit dictaphone, which pauses; the labelled « ✓ Terminer » / "Done" pill (aria « Terminer : relire dans le composer ») keeps everything and goes to review. The pill never shrinks: on the touch preset the C4 hint shortens to « Parlez et touchez » when the whole one would need a third line. Esc = « ✓ Terminer ». `pm-composer.tsx`, `use-pm-panel.ts`.
- `pm-pins`: picks through the public feedback picker: select never activates the element, a second tap removes, numbers stay in order, pins (24px, the element's top-right corner, the element's radius) show only on their screen and only over the page. A pick's label is its accessible name, else its own heading, its visible text, then the nearest heading around it (40 characters, read through the privacy policy, so masked text never becomes a label); its screen is the AppShell nav label (« Ma journée »), else `document.title`. The overlay's `labels: 'corner'`, `shape: 'element'`, `clip` and `mount` options are in `packages/feedback/src/page/picker/overlay.ts`. `use-pm-panel.ts`, `point-state.ts`.
- `pm-review`: « ✓ Terminer » transcribes and merges each pick after the last word ending at or before its time (picks before the mic go first); editable atomic chips (Backspace removes a chip and renumbers), the meta line « Dicté · 0:14 · 2 captures · modifiable ». `point-state.ts`, `inline-point-input.tsx`.
- `pm-one-line`: `improve={{ agent: 'pm' }}` is the app's only PM code: on the first open the kit reads `GET api/pm/agent` (identity), picks or creates the viewer's conversation at `api/pm/conversations`, opens `useRemoteChat` on `api/pm/chat` (same origin, the door of hachej/boring-factory#854); dictation is the host's, at `POST api/host/transcribe?language=` (audio body, `{ text, segments? }`). Every path is relative to the page (`document.baseURI`), like the app's own requests, so an app under `/apps/<app>/` reaches its own routes. `registry/pm-panel/use-improve-agent.ts`.
- `pm-one-line-tasks`: the one line's requests come from the host's task API: `GET _tasks/api?mine=1` → `{ tasks, cursor }` feeds « Mes demandes » and C7's request card (the reply whose turn ran `tasks_create`, JSON `{ created, task: { id, number } }` in its first text block, hachej/boring-factory#892; fallback: a `*task*` tool with a D-ref or id); « Confirmer » is `POST _tasks/api/<id>/answer { text }`, « Modifier » refills the composer; 404, 403 or a network error: « unavailable for now », never latched, read again on the next PM turn and when the panel or « Mes demandes » opens (`StudioTaskBoardSeam.refresh`). One adapter: `registry/pm-panel/pm-task-source.tsx`.
- `pm-request-from-tool`: C7's request card is drawn straight from the `tasks_create` result (`{ created, task: { id, number, title, state, status, updated_at, waiting_on_you?, confirmed_at?, is_requester? } }`), even before or without the list; when the list answers, its record is merged in and wins on conflicts. Without `waiting_on_you`: « DEMANDE », the title, no pill and no actions. `pm-task-source.tsx` (`requestRecord`).
- `pm-request-sent`: after « Confirmer », the card shows « Envoyée » at once and keeps it while the host's task carries `confirmed_at` and stays `open`; nothing new once work starts. Without `confirmed_at`, today's card.
- `pm-request-withdraw`: in « Mes demandes », the requester's card (`is_requester === true`, `open` or waiting on them) has « Retirer »; one tap asks « Retirer cette demande ? » (« Retirer » / « Annuler »), the second is `POST _tasks/api/<id>/withdraw` and the card leaves the list. 404 or 409: one muted line « Impossible de retirer la demande. », and on 409 the tasks are read again. Never on a card the viewer did not file, nor in Studio's board.
- `pm-member-thread`: the app panel's thread (C7) is the member's: `PiChat presentation="natural"` in `Panel` (no tool call, tool result or raw error; one status while the PM works), the PM's reply drawn as Markdown (`pi-chat-markdown`, headings at body size), and a run that ends in error without a reply shows one muted line (`PmText.failed`). Studio's `PmChat` keeps the builder view. `registry/pm-panel/pm-panel.tsx`.
- `pm-steps`: under each sent message of the app panel, the turn's calm step list (decided 2026-10-29): one muted 13px line per tool call in plain words (`PmText.steps`: « Lecture des tâches », « Création de la demande », « Mise à jour de la demande », « Lecture des fichiers », « Rédaction d'un document », « Préparation d'une maquette », « Question pour vous »; any other tool « Étape en cours »), a spinner while running, a check when done, a neutral dot when failed or unfinished; never a name, argument, result or error. Open while the PM works, folded to « n étapes ▸ » once reply text arrives or the run ends; a tap opens it. Before the first step: one « Préparation… » line. The reply streams as Markdown in the C7 type (`pmReplyText`). `registry/pm-panel/pm-steps.tsx`, `pm-panel.tsx` (`pmEntries`), `point-message.tsx` (`plainMessage`).
- `pm-mockups`: `present` results in the app panel are thread content: one row per mockup (a vertical list), a live thumbnail of the page in a sandboxed frame (scripts only, opaque origin, no network), the title and « Ouvrir › »; a tap opens it full height in the sheet under « ‹ Retour ». The file is read with `ImproveSpec.readArtifact`; the one line reads it through the door, `{base}/resources` (the revision route). `ask_user` keeps the question card, options and the free-text field at 44px. A round's text beside its tool calls stays. `registry/pm-panel/pm-mockups.tsx`.
- `pm-comment`: click to comment (desktop preset; in the app at web width without a coarse pointer, spec 1 §3b, board D7c): in `point.idle` a click picks and opens a 320px popover 8px below the pick (above when there is no room, inside the app's frame) with focus in the field; Enter sends the comment and that one pick (`pm.points@1`, its capture) straight to the PM as its own message, the draft untouched, the strip stays and reads « Envoyé au PM » for 3s; « Ajouter au message » (remembered per viewer in `localStorage`) or a click outside with text puts the chip and the text in the draft; Esc or a click outside with an empty field cancels the pick; while recording a click only pins. `registry/pm-panel/point-comment.tsx`, `use-pm-panel.ts`.
- `pm-back`: the panel owns Back like a layer: one history entry while it shows (sheet or strip), one more for the full panel, so Back goes full → sheet → closed and never leaves the page. `registry/app-shell/agent-layer.tsx`.
- `pm-panel-layout`: `AppShell improve={…}`: the first account-menu item, the sheet (C2/C6, the tab bar hidden), the 60px strip above a visible, working tab bar (C3–C5), and the full panel from 40px (C7) with the thread, the request card (`StudioTaskBoardSeam.renderRequest`, the host's `TaskRequestCard variant="compact"`) and « Mes demandes » (the `task-board` item's `TaskBoard variant="compact" filter="mine"` over `ImproveSpec.tasks`). The draft and pins persist across × and reopen. `pm-panel.tsx`, `registry/app-shell/account.tsx`.
- `pm-chat`: `PmChat`, the same conversation and composer for a Studio column (desktop preset), the point layer in the host's app frame. `pm-panel.tsx`.
- `pm-task-mentions`: `@` opens « TÂCHES · {query} » above the composer, the typed `@d-2` shows as an inline token (Composer `markMentionQuery`): the viewer's tasks (`filter: 'mine'`), at most 6 rows, « À vous » when the task waits on the viewer, ↑/↓/Enter/Tab/Esc; the `@ D-201 · Title` chip opens the task. Search, chip and opening are the shared `task-mentions` item's `taskMentions`; `pm-mentions.tsx` adds only the D9 header, rows and limit. `pm-mentions.tsx`, `registry/task-mentions/task-mentions.tsx`, `registry/pi-chat/composer.tsx`.
- `pm-send`: the message carries the text with chip markers, one PNG capture per pick and `pm.points@1` metadata (`n`, `kind`, `label`, `screen`, `selector`, `t`, `dictatedMs`); the draft, picks and pins clear on admission. `use-pm-panel.ts`, `point-message.tsx`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

In the app: account menu, « Améliorer l'app ». Type, or tap the arrow (« Montrer ») and tap what you want to show; the microphone records while you tap, the hand lets the app navigate, « ✓ Terminer » gives the draft back to review, send opens the thread. In Studio: the same composer at the bottom of the conversation column, pointing inside the app frame.

## Gotchas

The Factory PM door's watch is blocked by #854: the fixtures use the kit remote transport with a scripted model, a fake microphone and a fixed transcript. The C7 request card in the fixture is the shared `TaskRequestCard variant="compact"` with the host's « Modifier » / « Confirmer »; pm-panel ships no card or board. With the one line, the request card and « Mes demandes » come from the host's task API (`_tasks/api`, the contract may still shift: `pm-task-source.tsx` is its one adapter); @ tasks need a host `ImproveSpec.tasks`. App chrome (account sheet title, header avatar, tab bar) is AppShell's and differs from the boards.
