# App shell

The `app-shell` block gives an app its navigation, one header per screen, an account menu and a place for the agent, on a phone and on a wide screen.

## Sub-features

- `shell-layouts` three layouts: `screen`, `workspace` (list and detail, left pane: the icon rail below 1280px or on a screen without a `sidebar`, open otherwise on a first visit; see `shell-sidebar-open`) and `chat` (the page is `AgentWorkspace`). `registry/app-shell/app-shell.tsx`.
- `shell-nav` bottom tab bar on a phone (four items, more give three plus « Plus »), `compactNav="sheet"` for a sheet instead, shadcn sidebar with `rail="icon" | "hover"` on a wide screen, whose inset Separator sizes within its margins (no masked internal x overflow); `NavItem.placement: "before-sidebar"` puts an item above the screen's own sidebar content and first in the tab bar (D-1210). `registry/app-shell/nav.tsx`.
- `shell-header` one header per screen through `useScreenHeader`, back arrow, at most two actions. `registry/app-shell/header.tsx`.
- `shell-list-detail` `ListDetail`: both panes wide, one at a time on a phone with Back, collapsible list. `registry/app-shell/list-detail.tsx`.
- `shell-account` account sheet and menu: name, email, app menu, theme switch, sign out. `registry/app-shell/account.tsx`.
- `shell-sidebar-toggle` the collapse control sits in the sidebar header (top of the rail: next to the title expanded, alone as an icon when folded), never in the screen header on a wide screen; Ctrl+B still folds it and its name stays « Toggle Sidebar » (`data-slot="sidebar-trigger"`). `rail="hover"` has no toggle on a wide screen: the rail opens on hover and focus by itself, and has the pin of `shell-sidebar-pin` (the phone sheet keeps its close control). The rule (approved by the designer): the toggle moves inside the sidebar only where the rail exists (md+); on a phone there is no rail while the sidebar is closed, so with `compactNav="sheet"` its opener stays in the screen header (and the open sheet carries the same control as its close). `registry/app-shell/nav.tsx`, `header.tsx`.
- `shell-flat` the shell is flat: grey canvas and sidebar, one white `bg-card` content surface, no ring, border or shadow on the frame, no line under the header and no tint on the `ListDetail` detail pane. The kit declares no line at all: the phone tab bar's top Separator is deleted (it measured 0px in the pinned fixture, and the bar is told apart by its grey), the bar paints the rail's `bg-sidebar` (light and dark) and its items are inset 8px (`px-2`) from the screen edge, at 44px tap height; the sidebar edge is tonal; list row dividers belong to the app's own list; the vertical list/detail Separator measured 0px wide too and was removed. `ListDetail` takes an optional `empty` node for the detail pane while nothing is chosen. Its viewport values (`--app-shell-safe-top`, `--app-shell-safe-bottom`, `--app-shell-tabbar-min`, `--app-shell-sheet-max`) are tokens in the item's css, not arbitrary values. `registry/app-shell/`.
- `shell-sidebar-width` the sidebar width is a theme token (app look): an app sets `--sidebar-width`, `--sidebar-width-icon` or `--sidebar-width-mobile` on `:root` in its `theme.core.css` with no `!important`. The primitive carries the defaults (16rem; control height + 1rem, following the wrapper's own `--control-h`) as `var()` fallbacks resolved on the wrapper, so a sidebar without the app-shell css still has a width and a `SidebarProvider` `style` still wins. The phone sheet is 75% of the screen up to the Sheet's 24rem cap unless `--sidebar-width-mobile` is set (the old inline 18rem never applied). The dock + PM column breakpoint (`TOGETHER_FROM`, 1360px) assumes the default 16rem and cannot read the token; a themed sidebar wider than 16.5rem needs it raised. `shadcn/ui/sidebar.tsx`.
- `shell-sidebar-resize` (D-1898) `AppShell resizable` (off by default; `true` or `{ min, max }` in rem, 14 and 28: 224px minimum since D-1969): the sidebar's rail becomes the drag handle (`role="separator"`, `aria-orientation="vertical"`, `aria-valuenow`/`min`/`max` in rem, focusable) on a fine pointer from md; ArrowLeft/ArrowRight move it 1rem (toward the page: Right for a left sidebar), Home/End jump to the bounds, double-click returns to the theme's `--sidebar-width` and forgets the stored width. The width is kept in `localStorage` under `boring-ui:sidebar-width:<app>:<person>` (the app is `appId` when non-empty, else `title`; the person is `account.email`; with no email the key is the app's alone, so another person or another app on the origin starts from the theme width), clamped to min/max on read, every access in try/catch (a throwing store only loses persistence). The kit writes `--sidebar-width` on the wrapper itself; no app does it from a script (`design-no-script-tokens`). On touch or a phone the rail stays the toggle button and the sheet keeps `--sidebar-width-mobile`. Without `resizable` nothing changes. `registry/app-shell/sidebar-resize.tsx` (kit-owned; the host's stock shadcn sidebar is unchanged and gets no kit-only prop), `registry/app-shell/app-shell.tsx`.
- `shell-sidebar-resize-standalone` (D-1898) an app that renders `SidebarProvider` itself uses `ResizableSidebarProvider` (props `resizable`, `app`, `person`, then the host provider's own) and puts `ResizableRail` in its `Sidebar` where `SidebarRail` was; same handle, bounds, keys and storage as the shell (`useSidebarResize` is the hook under both). A drag cut short (release, cancel, lost pointer capture, Ctrl+B collapsing the sidebar) puts the inline transitions back. Drive it: `?standalone=1` in `test/fixtures/themes/sidebar-resize.jsx`. `registry/app-shell/sidebar-resize.tsx`.
- `shell-sidebar-open` (D-1909) the wide-screen left pane's open state, kit-owned around the host's SidebarProvider (controlled `open` / `onOpenChange`; the stock sidebar is untouched). With no stored choice, at 1280px and wider the pane is open when the screen on show has a `sidebar` content and the icon rail when it has none (the `screen` layout stays open); below 1280px, today's default (`rail !== 'hover' && layout === 'screen'`); the phone sheet and `rail="hover"` are unchanged. It is decided at mount (and when the person changes), never on a change of screen. Ctrl+B, the trigger and the rail are kept in `localStorage` under `boring-ui:sidebar-open:<app>:<person>` (the `screen` layout under its own `boring-ui:sidebar-open-screen:<app>:<person>`, D-1919: a fold in the workspace rail does not fold a screen pane and vice versa; "1" or "0"; the same `sidebarKey` helper and identity as `shell-sidebar-resize`: `appId` else `title`, `account.email`), written on a change only and into the key of the person who made it; junk is ignored and every access is in try/catch. `registry/app-shell/sidebar-open.ts`, `registry/app-shell/app-shell.tsx`.
- `shell-sidebar-pin` (D-2010) `rail="hover"` on a wide screen: a pin button (`data-testid="sidebar-pin"`, `aria-pressed`, one stable name « Épingler le volet » from `AppShellText.pin`, whatever the state) in the sidebar header. Hover intent (`HOVER_REVEAL_MS` 150, `HOVER_FOLD_MS` 300 in `nav.tsx`): the pane opens once the pointer has stayed 150ms on the rail and folds 300ms after pointer and focus left; keyboard focus opens it at once. Unpinned, the reveal floats over the page (its width and left do not change) on the sidebar surface with a shadow. The pin is the same stock ghost `Button` (size `icon-sm`) as the collapse toggle, with `aria-pressed`; pinned shows the filled Phosphor push-pin (`data-pinned` on the icon), and its tooltip reads « Épingler le volet · Ctrl+B » / « Désépingler le volet · Ctrl+B » (`AppShellText.pin` / `unpin`; the name stays `pin`). Shown when the pane is open by hover or focus (invisible but tabbable when folded on a fine pointer, so Tab reaches it and opens the pane; always shown on a coarse pointer, where a tap pins and opens). Pinned: the pane stays open whatever the pointer and focus do, the wrapper takes the pane's width so the page sits beside it (not over it, as hover-open does), Escape, blur, a click in the page and a portaled dialog, menu or popover opened from the pane do not fold it, and the screen's `NavItem.sidebar` is always rendered (never `collapsed`). Unpinned, the pane does not fold while a layer opened from it is open (its trigger has `aria-haspopup` with `aria-expanded`, `data-popup-open` or `data-state=open`); it folds once that closes with the pointer and focus outside. Unpinned, it folds only when both the pointer and keyboard focus have left. Unpinning folds unless the pointer, focus or an open layer is still there (the layer closing then folds it). Ctrl+B / Cmd+B toggles the pin on this rail, except in a text field, textarea, select or contenteditable, which keep their own Ctrl+B. Kept in `localStorage` under `boring-ui:sidebar-pin:<app>:<person>` (`sidebarKey('pin', ...)`, "1" or "0", same identity as `shell-sidebar-open`, every access in try/catch). No prop; `rail="icon"` and the phone sheet are unchanged. `registry/app-shell/nav.tsx`, `sidebar-open.ts` (`useSidebarPin`), `text.ts`.
- `shell-sidebar-action-touch` (D-1904) on a coarse pointer a `SidebarMenuAction` (the « … » of a row) is centred on its row and 44x44 with no extra hit area into the next row, a `SidebarMenuBadge` is centred too, and a row with both puts the count left of the action (the label truncates before both); a fine pointer keeps its offsets and sizes at every width, so the rule is the pointer, not `md`. `shadcn/ui/sidebar.tsx`.
- `shell-sidebar-sheet-row` (D-1972) in the phone nav sheet (the `Sidebar` in its portaled `Sheet`, 390 touch) a `SidebarMenuBadge` and a `SidebarMenuAction` are as tall as their row button and start at its top: the sheet content defines `--sidebar-row` like the provider wrapper. `shadcn/ui/sidebar.tsx`. Drive: `sidebar-menu-action.test.mjs` case `390-touch-sheet` (fixture `?mode=sheet`; shots `sidebar-action-390-touch-sheet-<theme>.png`).
- `shell-sidebar-subrow` (D-2020) a count or « … » in a sub-row (`SidebarMenuSubItem`) sits in the parent's right column (4px from the row edge) and on its own sub-row's centre, within 2px, at 1280 fine and 390 touch; on a fine pointer hovering or focusing a sub-row does not swap the parent's count (the swap follows the row's own button); a `SidebarMenuBadge` with `0`, empty or no children renders nothing, one that only wraps an empty `Badge` is hidden, and an empty `Badge` elsewhere (a status dot, the nav dot) is unchanged; in the phone sheet focus still moves inside on open, onto the sheet itself after a tap (no focus ring anywhere), onto the first item with its ring after a keyboard open, and the list hides its scrollbar on a coarse pointer yet scrolls. `shadcn/ui/sidebar.tsx`.
- `shell-sidebar-action-hover` (D-1960) on a fine pointer a row with a `SidebarMenuBadge` and a `SidebarMenuAction` shows the count at rest; hovered, focused within or with its menu open, the count hides and the « … » shows in its place (never both drawn at top 6, right 4). Touch is unchanged (`shell-sidebar-action-touch`). `shadcn/ui/sidebar.tsx`.
- `shell-sidebar-sub-size` (D-1961) `SidebarMenuSubButton` has the same text size as `SidebarMenuButton` (`text-xs`), fine and touch. `shadcn/ui/sidebar.tsx`.
- `shell-overlay-blur` (app look) overlays have no backdrop blur by default (D-1799): dialog, alert dialog, sheet and drawer only dim. `AppShell overlayBlur` (true) marks `<html data-overlay-blur="on">` and the overlays blur by `--overlay-blur` (4px). `shadcn/ui/{dialog,alert-dialog,sheet,drawer}.tsx`, `registry/app-shell/app-shell.tsx`.
- `shell-overlay-height` `height="auto" | "tall" | "full"` on `DialogContent`, `SheetContent` and `DrawerContent` (D-1800), emitted as `data-height`: tall is a max-height of `--overlay-height-tall` (85dvh) with the content scrolling inside; full is `--overlay-height-full` (100dvh; a Dialog less its 2rem margin; a Sheet on top and bottom, left and right are already full). `shadcn/ui/`.
- `shell-dock-load-focus` (D-1968) a wide dock that is already open at page load (the `?agent=` URL, a remembered open state) takes no focus: no header control, the Float button included, shows a focus ring on load. Focus moves to the panel's first dock control only when a dock control, a placement change or a side change strands it; Tab still reaches the controls with the usual `focus-visible` ring. Driven by `test/contracts/app-shell-render.impl.mjs` (« the wide dock opened at load takes no focus »). `registry/app-shell/agent-layer.tsx` (`DockPanel`).
- `shell-agent-trigger` the header's agent trigger (the coach mascot, `data-agent-trigger`) toggles the layer: a tap opens half (or leaves peek), a tap while open at half, full or docked closes it fully, two rapid taps from closed end closed. On a phone the sheet is modal, yet the trigger stays a reachable button (`aria-live="off"`, set only while the agent's own sheet is open at half or full, exempts it from that modal's aria-hidden; a Dialog still hides it) and, at full, the sheet stops under the page header so the trigger is not covered, also when the header grows later (D-1812). `AgentTrigger` in `registry/app-shell/agent-layer.tsx`.
- `shell-locale` (D-1900) `AppShell locale` reaches every kit part it mounts with no per-part prop: the layer, the pi-chat (placeholder, empty state, history), the account sheet and NavUser, the feedback banner. The order is `locale`, then `agent.chatProps.locale`, then the panel's `locale`, then English: when the shell sets `locale` it wins over `chatProps.locale` and the panel's, and a part's own locale applies only when the shell sets none (the layer header while a panel shows follows that panel's own locale before `chatProps.locale`). Drive: mount `<AppShell locale="en">` with an `agent`, open the layer (empty chat) and at 390 the account sheet; no string of the French label tables shows (`test/contracts/app-shell-render.impl.mjs`, « D-1900 »); shots at 390 and 1280 in en and fr: `test/contracts/app-shell-locale-shots.test.mjs` (CI artifact `app-shell-locale-shots`).
- `shell-screen-sidebar` (D-1902) in tabs mode on a phone, an item with a `sidebar` gets a leading header button (`screen-sidebar-button`: the item's icon, 44px, aria-label « <label>: <sidebarLabel> », `NavItem.sidebarLabel` or « Panel ») that opens the content in a left sheet (`screen-sidebar-sheet`, `--sidebar-width-mobile`, 75% by default); choosing a link, button or row closes it, a `data-keep-sheet` element or a field does not (judged from the click target, so an input inside a row keeps it open); a row that navigates keeps its navigation (the click is replayed after the sheet releases its Back entry); a Ctrl/Meta/Shift/Alt or middle click is not a choice (untouched, sheet stays open) and a second tap while closing is swallowed; a Back that never lands settles after `BACK_SETTLE_MS` (500ms, `registry/utils/back.ts`): the sheet closes, the held choice is dropped (never run ahead of the traversal) and the next row works; a choice still pending when the sheet unmounts (a resize to desktop, another screen) is dropped, never dispatched to a detached row; Escape returns focus to the button; Back closes it. An item without a `sidebar` has no button. The rule: whatever an item renders on desktop is reachable at 390. `ScreenSidebarSheet` in `registry/app-shell/nav.tsx`.
- `shell-nav-one-layer` (D-1903) on a phone with `compactNav="sheet"` the nav sheet closes when anything it holds opens another layer: the PM (Account > « Améliorer l'app »), the assistant, or another exclusive sheet such as the account sheet; one layer at a time, so a tap on the PM's Close reaches it. A sheet owned by the nav sheet's own content (a `NavItem.sidebar` with its own AppSheet) is nested: it stacks on the nav sheet (the `InsideNavSheet` context, set by `AppSidebar`) and closing it returns to the nav; only layers owned outside it replace it. Drive: at 390 with touch open the sheet, Account > Improve the app, tap `pm-close` (`CHROMIUM=... node --test test/contracts/nav-sheet-layer.test.mjs`; shots 1 to 5 in `.cache/evidence/nav-sheet-layer`, CI artifact `nav-sheet-layer-shots`). `registry/app-shell/nav.tsx`.
- `shell-back` one Back manager for point mode, sheets, the agent layer and list-detail stacks. `registry/utils/back.ts`.
- `shell-dense-targets` touch targets follow the `--control-h` / `--control-min` tokens (44px under 768px or on a coarse pointer, 36px on a fine pointer from md), `data-density="compact"` on a container forces 36px. `registry/README.md` section Agent layer.
- `shell-app-css` the app's base stylesheet is one kit file, `@hachej/boring-ui-kit/app.css`: type scale, colour map, defaults, base layer, shell/chat/feedback resets, overlay-blur rule; apps import it, never copy it, and the theme files imported after it win. `packages/ui/styles/app.css`, `registry/README.md` section "The app's index.css".
- `shell-theme-derived` the derivation of component tokens from the app's core roles is one kit file, `@hachej/boring-ui-kit/theme.derived.css`, imported after `app.css` and before the app's `theme.core.css`, whose roles and EXCEPTIONS win. `packages/ui/styles/theme.derived.css`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open any app that mounts `<AppShell layout=... />`; the example is `examples/app-shell/page.tsx`.
- Resize between phone and desktop widths; the same screens stay mounted.

## Gotchas

- Every screen stays mounted, hidden when not on show: state survives, effects keep running.
- `layout="chat"` with an `agent` prop throws on purpose.
- Never edit an installed block; `boring-ui-blocks check` fails on edited copies.
