TL;DR. Seven beads, strictly ordered, that make a hub restart survivable without touching the D29 gateway contract: a private harness seam, a ratified session key, a ledger that records what a model turn was doing, activity that rebuilds from it, and two falsifiable restart journeys. Reviewed by fresh-eyes (Opus) and a Sol cross-model pass — two P0s found and folded (the ledger did not record turn lifetime; the headless proof leaned on a store that deletes it). Nine decisions need your ruling; two change durable bytes.

GoalsNon-goals

  • A hub restart keeps pending owner gates ready and routes the answer once (#1348, #1413).
  • After restart the UI tells the truth: interrupted turns say so, paused turns show attention.
  • Two named proofs run as SIGKILL e2e journeys in both playground and full-app.
  • Store-backed replay, retention, real mid-turn transcript survival — P1-B.
  • Un-skipping Level D cases, flipping the flag, D29 addendum — P1-C.
  • Thread storage or ids (P2 shape spike), seatId semantics (P3).
  • Automation-run continuation across restart (its reconciler terminalizes orphans by design).
  • Pi rule 4 (Boring-owned model/credential authority) — BYOK lane, recorded as an open blocker.
