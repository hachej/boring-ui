D-2198 · AppShell layout · left: today (real kit 0.4.82 screenshots) · right: target (drawn with the kit's tokens)

Where the composer lives in the page

Same AppShell for every app, three regions. Sidebar (tabs on a phone): never moves. Content: the screen, with the one composer at its bottom. Right column: one thing at a time, the docked conversation or what the app opens (a file, a job, a record). Back to the composer.

1 · At rest

1280
TodayThe page draws its own bar (AgentBar) at the bottom. The shell's field lives only in the chat.
Target
The shell draws the one field at the bottom centre of the content, max 720 px. The page's scroll ends above it; the sidebar does not move.

2 · Open the conversation

Ouvert · after a send or the chevron
Today
No such state in Compta or Coach Max: opening the chat docks it on the right (row 3).
Target
The same box grows upward: the thread on top, the field below, one clean border around both, at the field's width (max 720 px), never the page's. About 30 % of the height, Agrandir to 70 %. The page above shrinks; nothing covers it. Échap or the chevron folds it.

3 · Docked

Ancré · « Ancrer à droite »
TodayThe assistant docks right with its own field. In Compta the page's bar stays at the bottom too: two fields on screen.
Target
Thread and field move together to the right column (~380 px, full height). Nothing stays at the bottom. « Détacher en bas » goes back to Ouvert, Fermer to rest.

4 · A file opened

dropped, attached or cited by the agent · conversation not docked
Today
The kit has no preview place; each app builds its own, and it competes with the docked chat for the right side.
Target
The file takes the right column; the conversation stays above the field (Ouvert) so you can talk about it. When the conversation is docked, see row 5. The kit gives the place; the app draws the preview.

5 · A file opened while docked

the conversation is in the right column
Today
The docked chat and an app's own preview compete for the right side.
Target
The file (or an artifact the agent made) opens in the same column, on top of the conversation. « ← Assistant » goes back to the thread; the file stays one tap away as a chip. The field stays at the bottom of the column the whole time, so you can talk about the file while you look at it. Nothing moves in the page.
