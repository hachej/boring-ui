D-2198 · the composer · left: today (real kit 0.4.82 screenshots) · right: target (drawn with the kit's tokens)

One composer, state by state

Today an app has two fields: the chat's composer (two rows, « + », Send) and the page's own bar (Compta, Coach Max). The target is one field, owned by the shell, one row, one action button. The pages around it: layout.

1 · At rest

nothing typed
TodayTwo fields. The chat's composer (top) and the page's bar (bottom). Each has its own buttons.
Target
One row: chevron (opens the conversation) · attach · the field · one action button, here Dicter because the field is empty. No « + », no Actions button.

2 · Typing

one line, then several
TodayFocus outlines the chat's box; Send turns dark once there is text. The bar keeps mic and send side by side.
Target
First character: Dicter becomes Envoyer, same place and size. Enter sends, Shift+Enter is a new line (on a phone, Return is Envoyer). The field grows upward to 6 lines, then scrolls inside; the buttons stay on the last line.

3 · Files

drop, paste or attach
TodayThe bar's chips float above the pill, outside it. The chat puts them inside its own box.
Target
Chips go inside the box, above the text. Uploading shows a spinner and holds Envoyer. A file alone can be sent. Tapping a chip opens the app's preview in the right column.

4 · Actions

« / »
TodayThe chat hides actions behind « + »; the bar has an Actions button; both open a list with its own search box.
Target
« / » typed in the field opens the app's actions above the box. Typing filters, ↑ ↓ moves, Enter picks, Échap closes and keeps the text. No second search box.

5 · The agent works

sending, then streaming
Today« Agent is working » (or « En cours » in the bar). It never says what the agent does.
Target
Conversation folded: a card in the same box says what the tool does, in its own words. The button is Arrêter. Unfolded, the same words show in the thread.

6 · The agent asks

a question, or a form of several
TodayTwo different cards. The bar shows only the first question of a form and answers the others « Pas de réponse ».
Target
The question sits in the box, above the field. A form asks each question in turn (« 1 sur 2 », then « 2 sur 2 » with the first answer), then sends them together. Typing in the field answers in your own words.

7 · A reply while folded

you went back to work
TodayA one-line status. The reply itself is not shown; you have to open the chat.
Target
The box does not open by itself. The reply shows in the box: « ✓ Terminé · what it used », 3 lines, the files it cites. A dot on the chevron until you open the conversation.

8 · Dictation

tap, or hold
TodayThe bar listens with ✕, a waveform, ■ and ↑. The chat has a separate voice-message recorder.
Target
Tap Dicter: ✕ · waveform · ■, the words show above as they come. ■ puts them in the field to review, then Envoyer. Hold Dicter instead: release sends at once, slide off cancels.

9 · Hold to send, on a phone

as in Coach Max (D5)
Today (Coach Max's bar)Press and hold the mic: « Relâche pour envoyer » and a waveform. Release sends; sliding off cancels. Only Coach Max's bar has it.
Target, every app
1 · Press and hold Dicter (past 300 ms): the row becomes « Relâche pour envoyer » and a waveform, and the words show above as they come. 2 · Release: the message is sent at once, with no review, and the box opens (Ouvert) with your bubble; the button is Arrêter. Sliding more than 24 px off the button, Échap or leaving the page cancels and sends nothing. A short tap keeps the dictate-then-review flow (row 8).

10 · A send fails

Today
The chat shows a notice above its box; the bar shows nothing. No screenshot.
Target
The text goes back in the field, the line above says what to do, Réessayer sends it again. Nothing is lost.

11 · Read only

a role that may only read, or no agent
Today
The bar is hidden; the chat is disabled without a reason. No screenshot.
Target
The box stays in place with one line saying why. Nothing can be focused.

12 · Edit last

the way Claude Code does it (D3)
Today
↑ does nothing. A sent message cannot be taken back; you send a correction.
Target
↑ in the empty field brings back your earlier messages as a draft (sent as a new one). A message sent while the agent works waits in the queue; ↑ or Échap pulls it back. Échap stops a run. Échap twice lists your messages: pick one, the conversation goes back to it (the old branch is kept) and it is in the field. On a phone: long press « Reprendre » or « Revenir ici ».
