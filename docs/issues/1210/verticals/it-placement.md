## Today / Delta

**Today.** The four verbs map unusually well (below). **B2B**, not B2C — a
materially better legal position than real-estate mandate prospecting.
Existing-client bench alerts fall closest to the existing-relationship exception.
**simap IT lots are genuinely in scope** (placement contracts routinely clear the
CHF 250k threshold), so the one platform with a sanctioned public API is actually
useful here. **Warm network** — owner's home market (Léman arc) has real
placement-firm density; a warm-intro pilot customer removes the risk-9 critical
path.

**Honesty flag / Delta.** Placement economics are **UNRESEARCHED** (web-research
budget exhausted before this vertical was added). Claims — Swiss placement margins
15-25% of day rate, a placement worth tens of thousands, Vaud firm density — are
**plausible and unverified**. Before this could displace the pilot it needs the
same research pass the trades got: mandate-flow channels, firm count, day rates &
margins, and what Swiss placement firms use today (**open question 9** — cheap to
close). The structural argument stands on its own regardless.
